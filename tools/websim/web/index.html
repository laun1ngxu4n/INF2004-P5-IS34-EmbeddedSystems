IS34 Robot Simulator

Your real firmware (app_program/, compiled to WebAssembly) driving a simulated Pico W on a Cytron-style tile track.

Tiles

Left-click / drag paints. Right-click rotates a tile. Tiles are 300 mm; barcode tiles are read going up when rotation is 0. A tile of the same type painted again updates its settings.
